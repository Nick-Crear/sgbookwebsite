Reading: The Silent Echo

Host: Elena Vance

The rain pounded against the windowpane, a relentless drumbeat matching the rhythm of her racing heart. She stared into the darkness, searching for a sign, any sign, that he was out there. But there was only the silent echo of her own thoughts, rebounding off the walls of the empty room. It had been three days since the incident, and still, the pieces of the puzzle refused to fit together.

Every shadow seemed to hide a secret, every whisper of the wind carried a hidden message. She took a deep breath, trying to steady her trembling hands, and reached for the worn leather-bound journal on the desk. The answers had to be in here, somewhere among the cryptic notes and faded sketches.

As she flipped through the pages, a loose photograph slipped out and fluttered to the floor. It was a picture of the two of them, taken years ago when everything was still simple. A pang of nostalgia hit her, sharply contrasted by the cold reality of the present. She picked it up, her thumb brushing over his smiling face, before tucking it safely back inside the journal.
